HYPERFRAMES ENGINE • OFICIAL
HTML puro.
GSAP pausado.
Render determinístico.
Mesmo t = mesmos pixels. Sempre. Em qualquer máquina.
data-* timing
paused:true
window.__timelines
<div data-composition-id="intro"
  data-width="1920" data-height="1080"
  data-duration="35">

  <div class="clip"
    data-start="0" data-duration="3"
    data-track-index="1">...</div>

<script>
  const tl = gsap.timeline({paused:true});
  tl.from("#title", {x:-40, opacity:0});
  window.__timelines["intro"] = tl;
</script>
