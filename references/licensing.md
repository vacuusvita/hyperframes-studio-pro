## Workflow

1. Pergunte onde será publicado (YouTube, IG, TikTok, TV, game, app?)
2. Abra licença atual de cada fonte usada
3. Cheque se use case permitido
4. Gere CREDITS.txt paste-ready
5. Flag sons sem licença conhecida
6. Flag música até licença confirmada
7. Flag conflitos (Mixkit music em TV/game)

## CREDITS.txt Template

```
CREDITS - video.mp4
Published on: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok (2026-10-14)

Music:
- "Upbeat Corporate" by Mixkit - https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/ - Mixkit Free License - used as background bed 0-30s

Sound Effects:

Licenses checked on 2026-10-14, all allowed for YouTube/IG/TikTok.
No known conflicts.
```
